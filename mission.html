The mission

Every team measures air temperature and pressure on the way down. RunnySat also points three sensors at the ground to tell healthy vegetation from dry, fire-prone scrub and burned earth.

01 · Primary mission

Measure the air on the way down, then land safely.

The primary mission is the same for every team: measure air temperature and atmospheric pressure after release and during descent, send the data by telemetry to the ground station, and land safely.

02 · Secondary mission · objective

Healthy, dry, or burned?

Our secondary mission tests whether a low-cost CanSat can tell healthy vegetation from dry, fire-prone scrub and from burned or bare ground during its descent.

7,958 haburned in Asturias in 2025, the highest figure since 2020
2,304 hain January–March 2026 alone, mostly scrubland

Fire risk depends on how much water live plants hold. The usual greenness index, NDVI, mainly tracks chlorophyll, so a green hillside can still be dry fuel. Any single sensor can also be fooled by shadows, wet soil, paint or artificial turf.

So we combine three independent measurements, and classify the ground only when they agree.

Healthy vegetation Stressed / dry scrub Burned or bare ground No agreement

Fire figures: ECOticias, 17 Apr 2026.

03 · Secondary mission · three sensors

Three ways of looking at the same ground.

Three downward-facing sensors look through the base of the can. Each one tests something different, so it takes more than a green colour to pass all three.

Click a sensor to see what it sees on the ground.

04 · Secondary mission · data path

Sort the view, send a summary.

A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, on its own switched 5 V supply, combines the three readings and sorts the view into healthy vegetation, stressed vegetation and burned or bare ground.

  3. XIAO ESP32S3 flight computerAdds GPS position and BMP280 altitude

05 · Secondary mission · validation

Test it on decoys before it flies.

Before launch we will calibrate the camera with a grey card and test the system on five targets. We expect only healthy plants to pass all three tests.

green gorsedried gorsecharred soilartificial turfgreen-painted card

The mission succeeds if

  • the three-sensor classification gives fewer false positives than NDVI alone, and
  • the flight data show the altitude at which a CanSat-sized probe can still detect these signs.

This is a proof of concept, not a fire-prediction system.

In the scene, watch the turf pitch drift under the can: NDVI alone calls it vegetation, but the red edge and thermal tests don't agree.
